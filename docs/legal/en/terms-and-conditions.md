## 3. Description of the service

Estilo is a technology platform that **connects** clients with barbershops and independent barbers in Colombia. Through Estilo you can:

- Search for nearby barbershops and view their services, prices, hours and ratings.
- Book, reschedule and cancel appointments.
- Receive appointment reminders.
- Rate and review the service received.
- Accumulate loyalty benefits offered by each barbershop.
- Manage a barbershop, its team of barbers, schedule, services and finances.

**Estilo is a technological intermediary.** The barbering service is provided directly by the barbershop or the barber, under their own responsibility. RC Studio does not provide barbering services, does not employ barbers and is not a party to the service agreement entered into between the client and the barbershop.

## 8. Appointments, cancellations and no-shows

### 8.1 Booking

When you book, you enter into a commitment with the barbershop. The appointment remains *pending* until the barbershop or barber confirms it.

### 8.2 Cancellation by the client

- You may cancel **freely and without consequences** up to **24 hours before** the scheduled time.
- Each barbershop may configure a more flexible cancellation window; in that case the barbershop's window applies and is shown at booking time.
- Cancellations within the 24 hours prior are recorded and may be considered for the reputation system described in section 8.4.

### 8.3 Cancellation or rescheduling by the barbershop

If the barbershop cancels or reschedules the appointment, the client may cancel with no consequence whatsoever, regardless of the time remaining. You will be notified in the App and, if the channel is enabled, by WhatsApp or push notification.

### 8.4 No-shows and temporary blocks

A **no-show** occurs when a client does not attend a confirmed appointment and does not cancel in advance. The barber or owner may mark the appointment as a no-show, respecting the arrival tolerance configured by the establishment.

Progressive consequences:

| Situation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 1st no-show | Warning in the App |
| 2nd no-show within 90 days | **7-day** temporary block from booking at that barbershop |
| 3rd no-show within 90 days | **30-day** temporary block from booking at that barbershop |
| Repeated pattern across several barbershops | Account suspension, with prior notice |

Blocks are **temporary and automatic**, limited to the booking feature, and carry no financial penalty. You may request a review of a block you consider unfair by writing to richerclarosdiaz@gmail.com; we will respond within a maximum of 15 business days.
