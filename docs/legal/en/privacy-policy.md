## 2. Definitions

- **Data subject:** natural person whose personal data is processed (you).
- **Personal data:** any information linked to, or that can be associated with, a natural person.
- **Sensitive data:** data affecting privacy or whose misuse may lead to discrimination (health, ethnic origin, biometrics, sexual orientation, among others). **Estilo does not collect sensitive data.**
- **Processing:** any operation on personal data: collection, storage, use, circulation or deletion.
- **Authorization:** prior, express and informed consent of the data subject.

## 10. Security measures

We apply reasonable technical, human and administrative measures to protect your data:

- **Passwords hashed with bcrypt** (one-way salted hash function). No one at RC Studio can read your password.
- **Encrypted HTTPS/TLS communication** for all traffic between the App and the server.
- **Short-lived JWT tokens** with versioning: changing your password invalidates all previous sessions.
- **Anti-bot rate limiting** on registration, sign-in, password recovery and file uploads.
- **Temporary account lockout** after several failed sign-in attempts.
- **Anti-fraud system for reviews**, flagging suspicious ratings so they do not distort the rating.
- **Row Level Security (RLS)** in the PostgreSQL database: every query is restricted to the rows the user is entitled to see.
- **Validation and sanitization** of all user input (protection against XSS and injection).
- **HTTP security headers** (CSP, HSTS) and a CORS policy restricted to authorized origins.
- **Principle of least privilege** for administrative access, with security event logging.
- **Encrypted database backups**.

No security measure is infallible. If you find a vulnerability, we appreciate a responsible report to richerclarosdiaz@gmail.com.

1. We will immediately contain and assess the incident.

3. We will inform you **by email and via a notice inside the App**, without undue delay and within **72 hours** of detection where the incident poses a high risk to your rights (arts. 33 and 34 GDPR).
