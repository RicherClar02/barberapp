## 3. How to delete your account from the app

1. Open the **Estilo** app and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab (👤 icon in the bottom bar).
3. Scroll to the **Legal and privacy** section.
4. Tap **"Delete my account"**.
5. Read the summary of what is deleted and what is retained.
6. **Confirm with your password** and type the word `ELIMINAR` in the confirmation field.
7. Tap **"Delete permanently"**.

Your session closes immediately and you will receive a **confirmation email** at the registered address.

> If you signed in with Google or Facebook and have no password, the app will ask only for the confirmation word.

## 4. How to delete your account from the web

You do not need the app installed.

1. Go to **https://appestilo.co/eliminar-cuenta**
2. Enter the **email address** you registered with.
3. Click **"Request deletion"**.
4. You will receive an email with a **confirmation link**, valid for **24 hours**.
5. Open the link and confirm. Deletion is executed at that moment.

This two-step process exists to prevent a third party from deleting your account without your authorization. If you never requested deletion but receive the email, simply ignore it: nothing happens without confirmation.

## 5. How to delete your account by email

Write to **richerclarosdiaz@gmail.com** **from the email registered on your account**, with the subject *"Account deletion request"*. We may ask for additional details to verify your identity. We will process the request within the deadlines in section 8.

## 6. What data is deleted

Upon confirming deletion, we **permanently and irreversibly** erase or anonymize all your personal data:

- ✅ Full name, email address and password.
- ✅ Phone and WhatsApp number.
- ✅ Profile photo (including the file at the image provider).
- ✅ National ID and date of birth, if provided.
- ✅ Department and city of residence.
- ✅ Notification preferences and push notification token.
- ✅ In-app notifications received.
- ✅ Conversations with the virtual assistant.
- ✅ Waitlist entries.
- ✅ Loyalty points and progress.
- ✅ Linked Google and Facebook identifiers.
- ✅ Security records associated with your account (registration IP, last access IP).
- ✅ The link between you and your historical appointments and reviews.

Your **reviews** stop showing your name and photo: they become anonymous, or are removed entirely if you expressly request it in the same process.

If you are a **barbershop owner**, additionally:

- Your establishments are **deactivated** and stop being visible to clients.
- The active subscription is **cancelled with no penalty**; no further charges are generated.
- Affiliated barbers are notified that the barbershop is no longer operating on Estilo.

If you are a **barber**, your professional profile is deactivated and disappears from the barbershop you were affiliated with.

## 7. What data is retained and why

Colombian law requires us to retain certain information. In every case, **it is unlinked from your identity**.

| Retained data | Reason | Period |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscription billing records** (amount, date, transaction number) | Tax obligation: art. 632 of the Tax Statute and art. 28 of Law 962 of 2005 | **5 years** |
| **Appointment records** without identifiable data (date, service, price, barbershop) | Barbershop accounting and business statistics | 5 years |
| **Aggregated and anonymous statistics** (appointments per month, a barbershop's average rating) | Service metrics. They **do not allow identifying you**, so they cease to be personal data | Indefinite |
| **Deletion record** (date of request and of execution) | Proof of compliance before the SIC | 5 years |

Billing data is retained associated with an accounting identifier, **not with your name, email or phone**.

## 9. Consequences of deleting your account

Before confirming, please note:

- ⚠️ **Deletion is irreversible.** We cannot recover the account or your history.
- ⚠️ Your **upcoming appointments are cancelled automatically** and the barbershop is notified.
- ⚠️ You lose the **accumulated haircuts** in the loyalty programs of every barbershop.
- ⚠️ If you register again with the same email, it will be a **new, empty account**.

If you only want to stop receiving messages, you do not need to delete your account: you can disable each channel in **Profile → Notifications**.

## 10. Alternative: download your data before deleting

We recommend **exporting your information before** deleting your account:

**Profile → Download my data**

You will receive a **JSON** file with all your personal data, appointment history, reviews and loyalty progress. This satisfies your right to portability (art. 20 GDPR and Law 1581 of 2012).

Once the account is deleted, this export is no longer possible.
